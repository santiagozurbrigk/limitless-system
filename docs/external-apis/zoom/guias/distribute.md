# App distribution

The Zoom App Marketplace is an ecosystem of Zoom-built and third-party apps available for users to connect with their Zoom account. By publishing an app on the Marketplace, your product or service is introduced to millions of daily users looking to connect workflows and discover new tools. Follow the submission steps in this section for a guide to publishing on the Marketplace.

> **Note**
>
> If you don't want to publish your app, you can select from the following:
>
> -   **Unlisted apps** might be a preferable option for you if you want to limit access to your app and don't want it available to the general public. For more information, see **Set my app as Unlisted** in [App discoverability](/docs/build-flow/app-listing/eu-and-discoverability/#app-discoverability).
> -   **Beta apps** are intended for testing. You can share them, with restrictions, by requesting an [authorization URL](/docs/distribute/sharing-private-and-beta-apps/#requesting-to-share-an-authorization-url).
> -   **Private apps** are intended for distribution to users within your Zoom account.

## Prerequisites

You have read [Before you start](/docs/build-flow/before-you-build/).

## Audience

Not all apps are intended for the general public; some are intended for use within your company or only for users in your Zoom account. If you choose to publish your app, you can select the intended audience. The review process differs depending if the app is for an internal or external audience.

-   **External Zoom users** can discover and install your app from the Zoom App Marketplace after it passes Zoom's review process. You can still limit installation to specific users, domains, or organizations.
-   **Users within my Zoom account** can discover and install the app only if they belong to your Zoom account. These apps are intended for internal use and don't require the standard Marketplace review.

## App submission, review, and testing process

To be published and released to the Marketplace, all public apps must undergo a dedicated App Submission Review process for app security, functionality and information.

In the first phase of the submission process, the Marketplace Operations team reviews metadata, usability, and compliance. The team works directly with you to meet all requirements.

In the second phase of the review process, to ensure customer data is protected and to ensure app security and resilience, the Marketplace Security team performs a Security Audit and a focused security test on Zoom scopes.

For more information about security testing, see [App Review Process - security](/docs/distribute/app-review-process/#step-3-security).

## Support and documentation

All developers are required to provide individual on-going support to users for their published apps. While Zoom provides active support for the use of Zoom products, all developers are required to actively provide users with their own dedicated support options for their published apps. Supporting your app includes providing, at minimum: a [Terms of Use](/docs/build-flow/app-listing/links/), [Privacy Policy](/docs/build-flow/app-listing/links/), [Self-Serve Documentation](/docs/build-flow/app-listing/links/), and [Support URL](/docs/build-flow/app-listing/links/) as part of the Zoom Marketplace Developer Agreement.
